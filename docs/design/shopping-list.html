Usability pass · #361 · for the owner's pick

The shopping list, easy to read

On the laptop the shopping list's tables stretch across the page, so a row's stock, length, and count sit far apart, and the guidance on choosing boards runs on below them as dense paragraphs. Two directions follow, on the phone and the laptop, grouped the way a trip through the store goes.

What to decide

  1. Which direction: L1 (suggested) narrow tables by store aisle with the choosing guidance folded, or L2 a checklist with no tables.
  2. Whether the guidance on choosing boards stays in the plan's shopping list, or lives only in Shopping mode's board cards (where it is already shown per board).

L1 · Narrow tables by aisle; guidance one tap away

Suggested
  • Three groups in store order: Lumber, Hardware (by the template's groups), Consumables.
  • Each table is as wide as its columns: Buy (count first, large), Stock, Length. Notes such as "Beam: a 10' board works if it measures 121-1/8"" sit under the table.
  • "Choosing boards" becomes a closed disclosure per group of parts, with the first line of each check showing.
