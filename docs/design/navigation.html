Usability pass · #356 · for the owner's pick

How the app is navigated

From the November 1 walkthrough, seven points that are one question: what the summary is for, how Shopping and Building are reached, where expert checks belong, where parameters and inputs live, where Tools and the glossary go, and what the phone's bottom bar holds. Three directions follow, each drawn on the phone and the laptop, then a critique and the questions to answer. Nothing is rebuilt until one is picked (PRODUCT.md, principle 6).

What to decide

  1. Which direction: N1 (suggested), N2, or N3.
  2. Whether expert checks move onto the step, check, or part they are about, with the summary's list dropped (all three directions do this; say if you want to keep the list too).
  3. Whether "Parameters and inputs" stays a plan section, or folds into Edit.

Where things are now

  • Phone: a bottom bar with Projects, Tools, and Glossary. A project opens on its summary; a Contents bar opens the eight sections; Shopping and Building are cards on the summary.
  • Laptop: a sidebar with the same three places, and the open project's eight sections under Projects.
  • The summary leads with four key figures (beam, legs, saw setting, lowest margin), then the two mode cards, then every expert check, then a one-line digest of each section.

IA-2 (#40) stays in every direction: the project is the plan, and Shopping and Building are full-screen modes launched from it. What changes is where the launch points sit, and what surrounds the plan.

N1 · The project header carries Shop and Build; Tools and the glossary go under Workshop

Suggested
  • Summary: gone as a section. A project opens on its first section that needs something (an input needed, else Diagrams), and the header shows status and what is next.
  • Shopping and Building: two buttons in the project's header, on every section, with progress beside each ("4 of 11 bought").
  • Expert checks: on the build step, structural check, or cut-list part each is about. The header's "next" line counts the open ones.
  • Parameters and inputs: fold into Edit, which already shows every value with its source; the plan keeps a link "Values used" in its contents.
  • Tools and glossary: under one place, Workshop: My toolbox, Glossary, Trash, Download all projects, theme, Log out.
  • Phone bottom bar: gone. The title bar has Projects (back) and a Workshop button; a project's sections stay in Contents.

N2 · A slimmer summary stays; the bottom bar keeps Projects and Workshop

  • Summary: stays as the project's front page, but leads with Shopping and Building and what is next; the four key figures move to the bottom as "At a glance".
  • Shopping and Building: the two cards, first on the summary, and nowhere else.
  • Expert checks: on what they are about, with a count on the summary linking to each.
  • Parameters and inputs: stays a section, last.
  • Tools and glossary: Workshop, as in N1.
  • Phone bottom bar: two places, Projects and Workshop.

N3 · Plan, Shop, and Build as the project's own bar

  • Summary: gone; a project opens on Plan.
  • Shopping and Building: while a project is open, the bottom bar becomes Plan, Shop, Build. The modes are still full-screen when entered, but entered from the bar.

  • Tools and glossary: Workshop, reached from the projects list's header.

N1 is suggested: it answers every point in the walkthrough, keeps navigation visible without a near-empty bar, and puts the modes where the owner already is. The glossary stays reachable from every marked term (#192) and from Workshop.
